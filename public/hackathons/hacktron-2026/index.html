About

Two weeks, online, and a prize pool aimed at things people actually deploy. HACKTRON is for teams who want to finish something: a working link, a repository, a short demo video and an honest write-up. No slide decks.

Judging runs against a published rubric with weights, assigned in advance, and results are published with the per-criterion breakdown. You can see the rubric before you register.

Who can take part Open to anyone. You need a Hackerly account to submit.

Rules

  • Teams of 1–4 people. A solo entry is fine.
  • Everything must be built during the event window. Using an API or a dataset that predates it is fine; shipping a pre-built product is not.
  • Your repository must be public, or reachable by the judges, from the moment you submit.
  • One submission per team. You may edit it freely until the deadline; the server refuses anything after it.
  • Judges cannot see each other’s scores, and neither can you. Only the published ranking reaches you.
