About

A physical hack night at the Meridian community hall in Leipzig. Bring a laptop, plug into the hall network, and build with the people sitting next to you. Everything runs on the local install, so the night keeps working even when the venue wifi does not.

Who can take part Open to anyone. You need a Hackerly account to submit.
This is a self-hosted event It is being run on a local Hackerly install, so the organizer controls the data and the judging. Nothing about it is shared with a central service.

Rules

  • Doors at 18:00, build from 18:30, demos from 22:00.
  • Teams of 1–3. Meet people at the door; organizers help you form a team in the first fifteen minutes.
  • Submission is a GitHub URL and a two-minute demo. No slides.
  • Judging happens in the room at 22:15 on the same projected screen you just presented on.

Prizes

€1,200 and hardware

Across 2 tracks. Individual track prizes are listed above.

Judging

Assigned judges score every project against the published rubric. Scores stay private until results are released.

Scores are private to each judge. Participants see the published ranking and a per-criterion breakdown once the organizer releases results (scheduled for 6 Oct 2026).
