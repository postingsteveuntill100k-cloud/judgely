About

Fold ran for 36 hours in May. Thirty-one teams entered, twenty-eight submitted, and the results below are the published ranking with the per-criterion breakdown. Everything on this page is the real output of the event, not a mock-up.

Who can take part Open to anyone. You need a Hackerly account to submit.

Rules

  • Everything built during the weekend.

Judging

Assigned judges score every project against the published rubric. Scores stay private until results are released.

Scores are private to each judge. Participants see the published ranking and a per-criterion breakdown once the organizer releases results (scheduled for 18 May 2026).
