About

This event comes from the shared DOGFOOD fixture set. It exists so a portal can be checked against realistic, awkward data: forty projects, thirty judges, uneven review batches and a scoring pattern that never varies.

Who can take part Open to anyone with an account on Hackerly.

Pick the track your project belongs to when you submit. Judges score against the same rubric whichever track you enter.

Schedule

  1. 30 Jan · 18:00 Registration opens
  2. 15 Feb · 18:00 Hacking starts
  3. 1 Mar · 18:00 Submissions close The server refuses anything later.
  4. 6 Mar · 18:00 Judging ends

Rules

  • Submissions closed on the date shown on this page.
  • The backend refuses any submission after the deadline, whatever the browser does.
  • Judges cannot see each other's scores.

Prizes

No prize pool

Across 8 tracks. Individual track prizes are listed above.

Judging

Each project is scored against the published rubric by the assigned judges.

Scores are private to each judge. Participants see the published ranking and a per-criterion breakdown once the organizer releases results .
