Browse without an account

Every event page, track list and project is public. You only sign in when you want to do something: register, create a team, submit.

02

Build with your team

Create a team or join one with an invite link. Keep the repository, the demo and the write-up together, and submit once before the deadline.

Get judged against a rubric

Judges score the criteria the organizer published, with weights. Scores stay private. When results are released you see the ranking and the breakdown.
